## What it is

Everyone in the group logs their own miles, and the whole party advances along the trail together, from Springer Mountain in Georgia to Katahdin in Maine.

- An interactive trail map with landmark stories
- A pixel-art camp that grows with the miles
- Badges for every hiker
- A wildlife-spotting minigame

It began life as *Oregon Trail DLE*.
