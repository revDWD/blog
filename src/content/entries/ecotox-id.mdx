## What it is

A quiz that plays like an old battle screen. Each round shows a real organism, photographed by someone on iNaturalist, and you name it. Get it right and it goes into your Field guide.

The species are chosen from the ones that matter in ecotoxicology: crop pests, pollinators, natural enemies and the standard test species.

<Note kind="aside">Offline-friendly on purpose: field days don't come with good signal.</Note>
